How it works — the 6 signals
Noise uniformity
Real cameras produce irregular sensor noise. AI diffusion models synthesize texture procedurally, creating unnaturally uniform noise patterns across image regions.
Color gradient smoothness
AI models interpolate color in ways optical lenses don't. Real photos have micro-transitions and optical variation; AI outputs blend too cleanly.
High-frequency detail bias
Latent diffusion models struggle with fine repetitive texture — hair strands, fabric weave, foliage. This signal checks how high-frequency content distributes.
Edge coherence
AI edges are mathematically "too clean." Real lenses produce bokeh falloff and optical blur; diffusion model edges are unnaturally smooth and consistent.
Saturation clustering
AI has an aesthetic tendency to hyper-saturate dominant hues while compressing mid-tone variation. Real photos have wider, less clustered saturation spread.
Output dimension pattern
Most AI generators output at fixed sizes (512, 768, 1024, 1536px). Cameras produce sensor-native resolutions that rarely align with these standard AI output dimensions.
